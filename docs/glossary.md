# Glossary

**ADME** — Absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion: four broad processes used to describe how a substance moves through and is handled by the body.

**Area under the curve (AUC)** — The area beneath a concentration-time profile over a defined period. Its interpretation depends on the sampling window and the context of the study.

**Bioavailability** — The fraction of a substance that reaches systemic circulation unchanged.

**Clearance** — A measure of how efficiently a substance is removed from a defined fluid, commonly plasma, expressed as volume per unit time.

**Compartment model** — A simplified mathematical representation of the body as one or more connected spaces. A compartment is a modelling abstraction, not necessarily a single organ.

**Concentration-time profile** — A description or graph showing how a measured concentration changes over time.

**Cmax** — The maximum concentration observed or predicted in a defined profile.

**Distribution** — Movement of a substance between circulation and body tissues.

**Elimination** — The combined processes by which a substance is removed from the body, often involving metabolism and excretion.

**Elimination half-life** — The time needed for concentration to fall by half during a phase in which the relevant elimination behaviour supports that interpretation.

**Excretion** — Physical removal of a substance or its metabolites from the body.

**First-pass effect** — Metabolism that occurs before a substance reaches systemic circulation, often involving the gut wall and liver after oral administration.

**Metabolism** — Chemical transformation of a substance by biological systems.

**Nonlinear pharmacokinetics** — Behaviour in which concentration or exposure does not change in a simple proportional way with changes in input, often because biological processes or binding are capacity-limited or otherwise variable.

**Pharmacodynamics** — The study of what a substance does to the body, including its effects and mechanisms of action.

**Pharmacokinetics** — The study of how the body handles a substance over time.

**Tmax** — The time at which Cmax occurs in a defined concentration-time profile.

**Uncertainty** — The limits of what is known about a parameter, measurement, model structure, or predicted result.

**Volume of distribution** — A calculated parameter relating the amount of substance in the body to its measured concentration in plasma or blood. It is not a literal body volume.
