# References and further reading

These sources are starting points for understanding pharmacokinetics, responsible model development, software licensing, and relevant Nigerian legal frameworks. Check the current version and applicability before relying on them in formal work.

## Pharmacokinetics and scientific practice

1. **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** — Clinical Pharmacology resources and guidance documents.  
   https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/clinical-pharmacology
2. **National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI Bookshelf)** — Open biomedical textbooks and review material.  
   https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/
3. **World Health Organization (WHO)** — Publications and resources on chemical safety and public health.  
   https://www.who.int/health-topics/chemical-safety
4. **International Council for Harmonisation (ICH)** — Guidelines relevant to nonclinical and clinical development.  
   https://www.ich.org/page/efficacy-guidelines

## Software licensing and dependencies

5. **GitHub Docs — Licensing a repository** — Guidance on selecting and applying a repository licence.  
   https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository
6. **Choose a License — No License** — Explains the default-copyright implications of publishing code without a licence.  
   https://choosealicense.com/no-permission/
7. **RDKit licence** — The project states that RDKit is released under a BSD licence; verify the exact version and notices for any distribution.  
   https://github.com/rdkit/rdkit/blob/master/license.txt

## Nigerian legal and regulatory starting points

8. **Nigerian Copyright Commission — Copyright Act, 2022**  
   https://copyright.gov.ng/CopyrightAct/
9. **Nigeria Data Protection Commission — Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023**  
   https://ndpc.gov.ng/download/nigeria-data-protection-act-2023
10. **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission — Resources & Library** — Official materials for the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 and related guidance.  
    https://fccpc.gov.ng/resources-library/

These links are not a substitute for legal advice. The repository has not yet received a jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## Citation practice

For every scientific claim, include author or organisation, title, publication date/version where available, and a stable URL or DOI. Prefer primary research and official guidance for technical claims. A source that reports a parameter in one population should not be treated as proof that the same value applies to all populations or exposures.
