# Contributing to Toxsim

Thanks for taking an interest in the project. Contributions should make the science clearer, the assumptions easier to inspect, and the limitations harder to miss.

## Good contributions

- Improve explanations of pharmacokinetic concepts.
- Add properly cited, accessible scientific references.
- Clarify model assumptions, parameter definitions, units, and data provenance.
- Add documentation about uncertainty, reproducibility, and validation.
- Correct factual or editorial errors and explain the basis for the correction.

## Scientific standards

For scientific claims, provide a reliable source. Prefer peer-reviewed literature, recognised textbooks, and guidance from relevant public-health or regulatory bodies. Clearly distinguish established evidence from a hypothesis, an inference, or a model assumption.

When discussing numerical parameters, include their units and context. Do not present values as universally applicable when they depend on population, route, formulation, measurement method, or other conditions.

## Safety boundaries

Contributions must not add instructions for poisoning, harmful dose optimisation, toxic mixture planning, or predicting how to cause a particular injury. Do not present model outputs as personal medical advice or as a substitute for professional toxicology assessment.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The change has a clear research or educational purpose.
- [ ] New scientific claims have citations.
- [ ] Units, assumptions, and limitations are stated where relevant.
- [ ] The wording is understandable to readers who are new to the topic.
- [ ] The contribution stays within the project's safety boundaries.

## Style

Write plainly. Prefer specific explanations over grand claims. Avoid implying that a model is validated, clinically useful, or accurate unless there is evidence that supports that claim.
